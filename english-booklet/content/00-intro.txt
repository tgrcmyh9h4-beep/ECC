# How to Use This Book

## Welcome!
Hello, and welcome! If you think your English is "very bad", that is okay. Every person who speaks English well today was once a beginner. Nobody is born speaking English. People learn it, one word at a time, one day at a time. You can do the same.
This book is written in **simple English**. The sentences are short. The words are easy. When we use a new or difficult word, we explain it. You do not need a teacher to use this book. You only need the book, a notebook, a pen, and about **30 to 60 minutes every day**.

## What is inside this book?
This book has eight parts:
- **Part 1: Getting Started** – how to study, and the sounds of English.
- **Part 2: Grammar Foundations** – nouns, articles, pronouns, adjectives, the verb "be", and more.
- **Part 3: The 12 Tenses** – how to talk about the past, the present and the future.
- **Part 4: Building Sentences** – questions, negatives, modal verbs, conditionals, passive voice and more.
- **Part 5: Vocabulary** – more than 1,500 useful words, phrasal verbs, idioms and collocations.
- **Part 6: Speaking and Conversation** – real phrases for real life: shops, work, travel, the doctor, the phone.
- **Part 7: Reading, Listening and Writing** – short stories, emails, letters and paragraphs.
- **Part 8: Your Fluency Plan** – a 90-day plan, daily habits, and how to keep improving.
At the end there is an **Answer Key** for every exercise.

## How each lesson works
Every grammar lesson follows the same pattern:
- **What it is** – a simple explanation.
- **How to make it** – a table with the form.
- **When to use it** – clear rules with many examples.
- **Common mistakes** – the errors learners make, and the correct way. Look for ✗ (wrong) and ✓ (right).
- **Practice** – exercises. The answers are at the back of the book.
> **Tip:** Yellow boxes like this one give you extra advice. Read them carefully. They help you remember.

## Seven rules for learning English fast
### Rule 1: A little every day is better than a lot one day
Studying 30 minutes every day is much better than studying 5 hours on Sunday. Your brain learns by repetition. Make English a daily habit, like brushing your teeth.
### Rule 2: Learn phrases, not only words
Do not learn only "decision". Learn "**make a decision**". Do not learn only "attention". Learn "**pay attention to**". Native speakers think in groups of words. When you learn groups, you speak faster and more naturally.
### Rule 3: Speak out loud
Read every example sentence in this book **out loud**. Your mouth must practise, not only your eyes. Speaking alone in your room is a great exercise. Nobody is listening, so you cannot be embarrassed!
### Rule 4: Mistakes are good
Every mistake teaches you something. Children make thousands of mistakes when they learn to speak. They do not stop. They keep talking. Be like a child. Speak, make mistakes, learn, and speak again.
### Rule 5: Listen a lot
You cannot speak well if you do not hear English. Listen to English every day: songs, YouTube videos, podcasts, films, news. At first you will understand only a little. That is normal. Keep listening and your ears will learn.
### Rule 6: Review old lessons
You will forget things. This is normal. Every week, go back and review what you learned before. Look at your notebook. Read old examples again.
### Rule 7: Use English in your real life
Change your phone language to English. Write your shopping list in English. Think in English when you walk or cook. Describe what you see: "I see a red car. The man is crossing the road." Small habits make a big difference.

## Your English notebook
Buy a notebook only for English. Divide it into four sections:
| Section | What to write |
| 1. New words | The word, its meaning, and one example sentence that you write yourself. |
| 2. Phrases | Useful phrases you hear or read, e.g. "Could you say that again, please?" |
| 3. Mistakes | Your own mistakes and the correct version. |
| 4. Diary | Three to five sentences every day about your day. |
> **Tip:** Writing by hand helps your memory more than typing. Try to write a little every day.

## How fast can I improve?
This depends on you. If you study one hour every day and follow the 90-day plan in Part 8, you can expect big changes:
| Time | What you can do |
| After 30 days | Introduce yourself, talk about your day, ask simple questions, understand simple sentences. |
| After 60 days | Use all the main tenses, talk about the past and future, write a short email, follow slow conversations. |
| After 90 days | Have real conversations on everyday topics, explain your opinions, understand much more of what you hear. |
| After 6–12 months | Speak with confidence, understand films with subtitles, write clearly, keep improving on your own. |
Fluency does not mean "perfect". Fluency means you can **communicate easily**, without stopping all the time. Many fluent speakers still make small mistakes. That is fine!

## A suggested daily routine (45–60 minutes)
| Time | Activity |
| 10 minutes | Review: read your notebook and yesterday's lesson. |
| 20 minutes | New lesson from this book. Read examples out loud. |
| 10 minutes | Do the practice exercises. Check the answer key. |
| 10 minutes | Listen to something in English (a song, video or podcast). |
| 5 minutes | Write 3–5 sentences in your diary. |

## Set your goal
Before you start, answer these questions in your notebook:
- Why do I want to learn English? (job, study, travel, friends, films, confidence)
- What do I want to be able to do in 3 months?
- When will I study every day? (For example: 7:00–7:45 in the morning.)
- Where will I study?
Write your answers and read them when you feel tired or lazy. Your reason is your fuel.
EX: Getting ready
1. How many minutes per day does this book suggest you study? || 30 to 60 minutes
2. Is it better to study 5 hours on Sunday or 30 minutes every day? || 30 minutes every day
3. Should you learn "decision" or "make a decision"? || "make a decision" (learn phrases)
4. What do the symbols ✗ and ✓ mean? || ✗ = wrong, ✓ = right
5. What are the four sections of your English notebook? || New words, Phrases, Mistakes, Diary
