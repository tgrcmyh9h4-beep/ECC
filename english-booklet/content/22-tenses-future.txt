# Future Simple: Will

## How to make it: will + base verb
The form is the **same for all persons**.
| Positive | Negative | Question |
| I / You / She / They **will** (I'll) help | I **won't** (will not) help | **Will** you help? |
- I'**ll** call you later.
- She **won't** be happy about this.
- **Will** you marry me? – Yes, I **will**!
! She will goes. => She will **go**.
! I will to call you. => I will call you.
! Will you to come? => Will you come?

## When to use "will"
### 1. Decisions made at the moment of speaking
- "The phone is ringing." – "I'**ll** answer it."
- "I'm cold." – "I'**ll** close the window."
- "What would you like?" – "I'**ll** have the chicken, please."
### 2. Predictions based on opinion (think, believe, probably)
- I **think** it **will** rain tomorrow.
- You'**ll** love this film.
- He **probably won't** come.
### 3. Promises
- I **will** always love you.
- I **won't** tell anyone. I promise.
### 4. Offers
- I'**ll** carry your bag.
- **Shall** I open the window? (offer – British, with I/we)
### 5. Requests
- **Will** you help me, please?
### 6. Future facts
- The sun **will** rise at 6:15 tomorrow.
- I'**ll** be 30 next year.
### 7. Threats and warnings
- Stop or I'**ll** call the police!
- Be careful, you'**ll** fall!

# Future with "Going To"

## How to make it: am / is / are + going to + base verb
| Positive | Negative | Question |
| I'**m going to** study. | I'**m not going to** study. | **Am** I **going to**...? |
| She'**s going to** travel. | She **isn't going to** travel. | **Is** she **going to**...? |
| They'**re going to** move. | They **aren't going to** move. | **Are** they **going to**...? |

## When to use "going to"
### 1. Plans and intentions (decided before now)
- I'**m going to** buy a new laptop next month. (I've already decided.)
- We'**re going to** get married in December.
- What **are** you **going to** do after university?
### 2. Predictions based on evidence (what you can see now)
- Look at those black clouds! It'**s going to** rain.
- Be careful! You'**re going to** drop those plates!
- She's pregnant. She'**s going to** have a baby.

## Will or going to?
| Will | Going to |
| Decision **now** | Plan made **before** |
| "We have no milk." – "Oh, I'**ll** buy some." | "Why are you taking your bag?" – "I'**m going to** buy milk." |
| Prediction (opinion) | Prediction (evidence) |
| I think Brazil **will** win. | Brazil is 3-0 up. They'**re going to** win. |

# Present Continuous and Present Simple for the Future

## Present continuous for arrangements
When you have a **fixed arrangement** with other people (time, place – often in your diary), use the present continuous:
- I'**m meeting** my boss at 3 o'clock.
- We'**re having** a party on Saturday.
- She'**s flying** to Singapore tomorrow morning.
- What **are** you **doing** tonight?

## Present simple for timetables
For official schedules (trains, flights, films, classes), use the present simple:
- The train **leaves** at 6:45.
- The concert **starts** at 8.
- Our English class **begins** on 3rd March.

## Future time clauses: when, before, after, until, as soon as, if
After these words, we use the **present** (not will) to talk about the future.
- I'll call you **when** I **arrive**. (not "when I will arrive")
- **As soon as** she **finishes**, we'll leave.
- Wait here **until** I **come** back.
- **Before** you **go**, please close the window.
- **If** it **rains**, we'll stay home.
! I'll call you when I will get home. => I'll call you when I **get** home.

## Summary: talking about the future
| Form | Use | Example |
| will | decisions now, promises, predictions (opinion) | I'll help you. I think it'll be fine. |
| going to | plans, predictions (evidence) | I'm going to study medicine. It's going to rain. |
| present continuous | fixed arrangements | I'm seeing the doctor at 4. |
| present simple | timetables | The bus leaves at 10. |
| be about to | very near future | Hurry! The film is about to start. |
EX: Will or going to?
1. "I'm thirsty." – "I ___ get you some water." || 'll (will)
2. Look at the sky! It ___ rain. || 's going to
3. We ___ visit Kerala next summer. We booked the hotel. || 're going to
4. I think she ___ pass the exam. || 'll (will)
5. "The bag is heavy." – "I ___ help you." || 'll (will)
6. He ___ start a new job next month. He signed the contract. || 's going to
7. I promise I ___ be late again. || won't
8. Be careful! You ___ fall! || 're going to
EX: Correct the mistakes
1. I will to go to the shop. || I will go to the shop.
2. When I will finish, I'll call you. || When I finish, I'll call you.
3. She will comes tomorrow. || She will come tomorrow.
4. If it will rain, we stay home. || If it rains, we will stay home.
5. Are you go to study tonight? || Are you going to study tonight?

# Future Continuous

## What is it?
The future continuous is for an action that will be **in progress** at a time in the future.
- This time tomorrow, I'**ll be flying** to London.
- At 8 p.m. we'**ll be having** dinner. Don't call then!

## How to make it: will be + verb-ing
| Positive | Negative | Question |
| I **will be** working | I **won't be** working | **Will** you **be** working? |

## When to use it
### 1. In progress at a future time
- At 10 a.m. tomorrow, I'**ll be sitting** in an exam.
- Don't call at 7 – I'**ll be driving**.
### 2. Polite questions about plans
- **Will** you **be using** the car tonight? (Can I use it?)
- **Will** you **be coming** to the meeting?
### 3. Things that will happen as a normal course of events
- I'**ll be seeing** Rahul at work tomorrow, so I can give him your message.
EX: Complete with the future continuous
1. This time next week, I ___ (lie) on a beach. || will be lying
2. At 9 tomorrow, she ___ (teach). || will be teaching
3. ___ you ___ (use) your laptop this evening? || Will ... be using
4. Don't phone at 6. We ___ (have) dinner. || will be having
5. In 2030, people ___ (drive) electric cars. || will be driving

# Future Perfect

## What is it?
The future perfect is for an action that **will be finished before** a time in the future.
- By 2030, I **will have finished** university.
- By the time you arrive, I'**ll have cooked** dinner.

## How to make it: will have + past participle (V3)
| Positive | Negative | Question |
| I **will have** finished | I **won't have** finished | **Will** you **have** finished? |
Signal words: **by** (by tomorrow, by 5 p.m., by next year), **by the time**, **before**, **in two weeks' time**
- **By** next June, we **will have been** married for ten years.
- She **won't have finished** the report **by** Friday.
- **Will** they **have arrived** **by** 8?
EX: Complete with the future perfect
1. By 10 p.m., I ___ (finish) my homework. || will have finished
2. By next year, she ___ (save) enough money. || will have saved
3. The film ___ (start) by the time we get there. || will have started
4. ___ you ___ (read) the book by Monday? || Will ... have read
5. By 2030, they ___ (build) the new airport. || will have built

# Future Perfect Continuous

## What is it?
The future perfect continuous shows **how long** an action will have continued up to a point in the future.
- By December, I **will have been working** here for ten years.
- When she retires, she **will have been teaching** for 40 years.

## How to make it: will have been + verb-ing
- By 6 o'clock, we **will have been waiting** for three hours.
- Next month, they **will have been living** in Canada for five years.
> **Tip:** This tense is rare in conversation. You need to understand it, but you will not use it often. Don't worry about it too much!
EX: Complete with the future perfect continuous
1. By noon, I ___ (study) for four hours. || will have been studying
2. In June, we ___ (live) here for two years. || will have been living
3. By the time he finishes, he ___ (work) for 12 hours. || will have been working

# All 12 Tenses: Summary and Review

## One verb, 12 tenses
| Tense | Example with "eat" | Key use |
| Present simple | I eat rice every day. | habits, facts |
| Present continuous | I am eating now. | now, temporary, arrangements |
| Present perfect | I have eaten sushi. | experience, result now, unfinished time |
| Present perfect continuous | I have been eating for 20 minutes. | duration up to now |
| Past simple | I ate at 8 last night. | finished past |
| Past continuous | I was eating when you called. | in progress in the past |
| Past perfect | I had eaten before you arrived. | before another past action |
| Past perfect continuous | I had been eating for an hour when... | duration before a past point |
| Future simple | I will eat later. | decisions, predictions, promises |
| Future continuous | I will be eating at 8. | in progress in the future |
| Future perfect | I will have eaten by 9. | finished before a future time |
| Future perfect continuous | By 9, I will have been eating for an hour. | duration up to a future point |

## Questions in all tenses
| Tense | Question |
| Present simple | **Do** you **work** here? |
| Present continuous | **Are** you **working**? |
| Present perfect | **Have** you **worked** here long? |
| Present perfect continuous | How long **have** you **been working** here? |
| Past simple | **Did** you **work** yesterday? |
| Past continuous | **Were** you **working** at 9? |
| Past perfect | **Had** you **worked** there before? |
| Past perfect continuous | How long **had** you **been working**? |
| Future simple | **Will** you **work** tomorrow? |
| Future continuous | **Will** you **be working** at 5? |
| Future perfect | **Will** you **have finished** by 5? |
| Future perfect continuous | How long **will** you **have been working**? |

## Signal words help you choose the tense
| Signal words | Tense |
| always, usually, often, every day, on Mondays | present simple |
| now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!, this week | present continuous |
| ever, never, just, already, yet, for, since, so far | present perfect |
| how long, all day, for, since (with action verbs) | present perfect continuous |
| yesterday, ago, last week, in 2010, when I was young | past simple |
| while, at 8 last night, when (+ interruption) | past continuous |
| before, after, already, by the time (past) | past perfect |
| tomorrow, next week, I think, probably, soon | will / going to |
| this time tomorrow, at 8 tomorrow | future continuous |
| by tomorrow, by the time, by 2030 | future perfect |
